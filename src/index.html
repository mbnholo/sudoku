HOW TO PLAY

Enjoy the logic.

Fill the grid with numbers from 1 to 9 without repeating a number in any row, column, or 3×3 box.

  • Notes: mark possible numbers in an empty cell.
  • Hint: reveal a correct number.
  • Undo: return to your previous move.
  • New: start another puzzle at the selected difficulty.

Play offline on iPhone

Open this site in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, and enable Open as Web App if available. Open the icon at least once while online before trying airplane mode.

Games are saved only on this device. Clearing site data may erase them. There is no sync between devices.
